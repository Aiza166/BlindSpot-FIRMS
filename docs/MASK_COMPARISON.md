# Persistent-hotspot mask: old vs new rule

Generated by `python -m blindspot.mask_compare` at 2026-10-31T16:27:08+00:00 from `hotspots_filtered` (standard data, type 0, confidence, coverage box, no duplicates). Do not edit by hand. Months and years are local solar time. Platforms are never summed. Every detection in a masked cell (all years, all platforms) counts as removed.

- **Old** (`--rule seasonal-offseason`, D-022, superseded): cell has a detection in ≥3 distinct years during Jan–Mar or Jul–Sep.
- **New** (`--rule year-round`, default, D-024): in ≥3 distinct years the cell has detections in ≥4 distinct months that no two 3-month windows (wrapping Dec→Jan) can cover.

The rule was changed after the old rule's results were seen (CLAUDE.md rule 6); both are reported here.
