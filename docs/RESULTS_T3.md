## Caveats (numbers from t3.json)

- IN Punjab night counts are small: S-NPP night mean 20 per window in 2012–21 vs 70 in 2022–25, while day counts fell from 61,452 to 21,259. The night rise is far too small to offset the day fall, so a real decline (D) and a later-burning shift (T) can both be present; the rules can say T is supported, not that D is absent.
- The pre-drift curve misses the control: rest_of_box 2022–25 mean residual -23.6% (the curve predicts a rise the control does not show). The curve is fit on natural day-to-day Δ, which mostly reflects VIIRS swath position, not Aqua's clock time, so the orbit extrapolation is a weak instrument; compare each region's residual with the control's.
- No region has 2 post years with ≥ 10 close-overpass days, so the cleanest orbit-free comparison (A3 close) is insufficient everywhere.

## Reason codes

- `pre2012_terra_aqua_trend`: Terra/Aqua ratio trends in 2003-2011, so Aqua x factor may not be stable before VIIRS
- `splice_step_outside_normal`: 2012 harmonized step exceeds 2 sd of the 2013-21 factor
- `aqua_overpass_drift`: Aqua's overpass drifted > 30 min later; the Aqua-to-VIIRS factor cannot be checked this year
- `burning_time_shift`: part of the burning moved later in the day; 13:30 satellite counts may understate it (a real decline is not ruled out)
