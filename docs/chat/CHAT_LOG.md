## Idea 1: "Smog Season Calendar" (Hot Spots challenge) ⭐ my pick
**The pitch:** *"Every November, Lahore can't breathe. The fires behind it show up on satellites weeks ahead. We turned 25 years of fire data into a calendar that shows when smog season is coming."*

- **Core:** combine MODIS fire detections (since 2000, 1 km resolution) with VIIRS (since 2012, 375 m). VIIRS spots many more small crop fires, so the raw counts can't be compared. Use the overlap years, 2012 onwards, to fit a conversion for each region and season. Then build a calendar heatmap for any area of interest.
- **What sets it apart:** a **"Can you trust this?" panel** that shows the raw versus harmonized counts and where the conversion is weak. Most teams will just stack the two datasets on top of each other. Your leakage-audit habit is exactly the skill this needs.
- **Anomaly alerts:** for example, *"This week is 3× the 20-year norm for Punjab,"* written in plain Urdu and English by an LLM. The LLM only reads computed statistics through your MCP setup, so it can't invent numbers.
- **Demo moment:** scrub through 25 Novembers, then show *this* November, live during the hackathon.
