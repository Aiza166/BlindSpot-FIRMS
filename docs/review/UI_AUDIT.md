## Findings, most costly first
1. **The answer is not first.** (H8, Von Restorff) Explorer opens with a 40 px district name, then a card whose headline is a small "6" under a 13 px label; the answer to "is this month unusual?" (the ratio to the median) is in 13.5 px grey text. Fix: eyebrow "Is this month unusual here?" above the card, ratio-to-median set as a second prominent line beside the number, card gets a stronger border and accent edge. The numbers and sentences are untouched.
2. **Phone: the area is lost on scroll and controls are small.** (Fitts) `explorer-phone-dark.png` is ~8000 px tall; after the map the area name scrolls away; buttons, inputs and nav links are 28-36 px. Fix: sticky area name bar on phones (solid, not translucent), 44 px targets, thumb-reachable full-width actions.
3. **Calendar cells give no pressed feedback and no touch path.** (Doherty, A11Y #14) Hover-only readout; nothing happens on tap. Fix: `:active` press on pointer-down, tap sets the readout, tooltip anchored at the cell with a 100 ms fade.
4. **Area picker has no step order and no empty state.** (H6, empty-states) Fix: "1 Choose an area / 2 Choose a period" step labels, stage empty state, drawing works with touch.
5. **Dark theme polish.** `--control-border` missing under `data-theme=dark` (controls fall back to light-theme grey); card and plate edges nearly vanish. Fix: token added, hairline highlight, prefers-contrast:more strengthens borders.
6. **Type and spacing are ad hoc.** 40/38/26/24/22/20 px headings, 12.5/13/13.5/14/15 px small text, 18/20/22 px gaps. Fix: scale and 4/8 tokens (see skills above).
7. **Motion is nothing but a global kill switch.** Adds only subtle, interruptible transitions on controls and tooltip.

## Not changed on purpose
Caution flags, "Latest available month, not live", ODbL attribution, scope line, fail/inconclusive labels, cividis ramp, all data-derived text: same markup, same or stronger visual weight.

## After
| Area | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Month card | Answer in 13.5 px grey text under a small number | Eyebrow "Is this month unusual here?", number up to 52 px with -0.02em tracking, the all-years comparison line at 16 px / weight 500 in ink, 4 px accent edge; "not live" tag 13 px |
| Press feedback | None | Buttons, segmented control, map zoom, calendar cells scale on pointer-down (100 ms, transform only); tap sets the readout; tooltip fades in 100 ms, wraps and is clamped to the grid |

| Dark | `--control-border` fell back to light value under `data-theme=dark` | Defined; hairline highlight on cards |
| Type/space | 6 heading sizes, 15 px body | 16 px body, clamp() headings with -0.02em, weights 400/500/600, 4/8 tokens |
