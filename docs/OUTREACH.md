# Outreach drafts

Drafts for the team to send by hand. Nothing here is sent automatically. Send only after the app is public (14 Nov 09:00 PKT or later), and replace `{URL}` with the public link. Keep messages short; one ask per message.

Who: Rescue 1122 (Punjab Emergency Service) district officers, PDMA Punjab, agriculture extension officers (Punjab Agriculture Department), and researchers on crop-residue burning and air quality (universities, PMD, Punjab EPA).

## Rules

- Do not claim an endorsement. A quote is one person's view.
- Do not quote anything said in a private message without option 1 or 2 in writing.
- Do not share the MAP_KEY or any data file links other than the public app URL.
- Do not message private individuals in bulk; at most a few targeted people per group.
